# Botany sources

The profiles in `src/botany/species.js` (leaf outline, size and arrangement,
crown architecture, bark, flowers and fruit with their timing) come from these
fact sheets:

- Freeman / Autumn Blaze maple: [UMN Trees](https://trees.umn.edu/autumn-blaze%C2%AE-freemans-maple-acer-x-freemanii-%E2%80%98jeffersred%E2%80%99), [Morton Arboretum](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/freemans-maple/), [Missouri Botanical Garden](https://www.missouribotanicalgarden.org/PlantFinder/PlantFinderDetails.aspx?kempercode=c789)
- Redmond linden: [Purdue Arboretum](https://www.arboretum.purdue.edu/explorer/plants/680/), [UF/IFAS](https://hort.ifas.ufl.edu/database/documents/pdf/tree_fact_sheets/tilamec.pdf), [Morton Arboretum](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/american-basswood/)
- Bur oak: [Ohio DNR](https://ohiodnr.gov/discover-and-learn/plants-trees/broad-leaf-trees/bur-oak-quercus-macrocarpa), [Minnesota Wildflowers](https://www.minnesotawildflowers.info/tree/bur-oak), [MN DNR](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/trees/bur-oak.html)
- Norway spruce: [Go Botany](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/picea/abies/), [Virginia Tech](https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/HORT/HORT-20/HORT-20.html)
- Colorado blue spruce: [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/picea-pungens-glauca/), [Morton Arboretum](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/blue-spruce/)
- Black Hills spruce: [Morton Arboretum](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/black-hills-spruce/)
- Mugo pine: [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/pinus-mugo/)
- Honeylocust: [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gleditsia-triacanthos-var-inermis/), [Illinois Wildflowers](https://www.illinoiswildflowers.info/trees/plants/honey_locust.html)
- Parkland Pillar birch: [Wasco Nursery](https://www.wasconursery.com/product/parkland-pillar-japanese-white-birch/), [NVK Nurseries](https://www.nvknurseries.com/plant/Betula-platyphylla-Jefpark-Parkland-Pillar)
- Prairifire crabapple: [Chicago Botanic Garden](https://www.chicagobotanic.org/plant-information/plant-finder/malus-prairifire-prairifire-crabapple), [Oklahoma State](https://extension.okstate.edu/programs/oklahoma-proven/plant-profiles/prairifire-crabapple)
- Limelight hydrangea: [Missouri Botanical Garden](http://www.missouribotanicalgarden.org/plantfinder/plantfinderdetails.aspx?kempercode=c192), [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hydrangea-paniculata-limelight/)
- Arborvitae: [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/thuja-occidentalis/), [Ohio DNR](https://ohiodnr.gov/discover-and-learn/plants-trees/scale-like-leaves/arborvitae-thuja-occidentalis)
- Quaking aspen: [Go Botany](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/populus/tremuloides/), [USU Extension](https://extension.usu.edu/forestry/tree-identification/poplar-aspen/quaking-aspen)
- Golden weeping willow: [Morton Arboretum](https://mortonarb.org/plant-and-protect/trees-and-plants/golden-weeping-willow/)
- Hackberry: [Go Botany](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/celtis/occidentalis/)
- American elm: [Oregon State](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/ulmus-americana)
- Prairie Horizon alder: [NDSU Extension](https://www.ndsu.edu/agriculture/extension/publications/prairie-horizon-manchurian-alder)

Growth method: A. Runions, B. Lane, P. Prusinkiewicz, *Modeling Trees with a
Space Colonization Algorithm* (Eurographics Workshop on Natural Phenomena, 2007).
