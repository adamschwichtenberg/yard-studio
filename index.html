Import property boundary

Local image → review corners → name and measure a side → import boundary. Your image stays on this device.

Use a plan-view, to-scale diagram or orthorectified aerial. One side cannot correct perspective in an ordinary oblique photo or a survey marked “not to scale.” Results are estimates, not a legal survey. No OCR is performed: enter a printed length yourself.

Maximum 20 MiB, 8,192 px per dimension and 24 megapixels. Processing is limited to 1 megapixel / 1,200 px.

Choose an image to begin.

Image detection and boundary review

Settings apply on Detect again or Find enclosed region. Bridging thickens lines and can merge gaps; preview uses the inner edge of the outline. Buildings, text and page frames may also be candidates. Interior holes are not imported.

Drag corners to edit a closed boundary. While tracing, hover near Start (corner 1) to preview closure, then click or tap to close without losing corners. Enter or Finish trace also closes. Arrow keys move a focused corner by 1 processing pixel; Shift moves 10. Delete removes it.

Use Pan to scroll by touch without moving or adding corners. Side values update live.

Corners

Coordinates use the reduced preview image. Splitting keeps the old name on the first half; merging keeps both names. Editing the reference side’s topology clears its calibration. Undo restores it.
